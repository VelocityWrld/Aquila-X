# Research and References

## Purpose

This document records the evidence status of the Aquila-X concept. The repository currently contains supplied concept notes rather than a completed literature review or certification dossier.

## Source Material

The repository’s primary source is the consolidated Aquila-X concept document supplied for this project. It includes preliminary materials, features, ratings, sales estimates, noise-reduction ideas, ESC and dampener notes, and a prototype-to-post-prototype roadmap.

See the preserved source copy in [`archive/original-documents/Aquila-X-Complete-Notes.md`](../archive/original-documents/Aquila-X-Complete-Notes.md).

## Validation Sources To Add

Before treating the design as an engineering or operational proposal, the project should add authoritative references for:

- Applicable national and regional unmanned-aircraft rules.
- Radio-spectrum allocation, equipment authorization, and encryption requirements.
- Battery, propulsion, electromagnetic-compatibility, and environmental testing standards.
- Privacy and data-protection rules relevant to imaging and biometric processing.
- Supplier datasheets and integration manuals for selected flight controllers, ESCs, motors, batteries, sensors, cameras, and onboard computers.
- Independent test results for acoustics, vibration, endurance, thermal performance, and navigation reliability.

## Evidence Discipline

Every future quantitative claim should identify its source, date, configuration, test conditions, and uncertainty. Market estimates and performance projections should be labeled as assumptions until supported by documented research or measured results.

## Important Distinction

The presence of a component name or capability in the concept notes does not establish that the component has been selected, procured, integrated, legally approved, or tested. This repository intentionally preserves that distinction.
